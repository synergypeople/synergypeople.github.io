1. Purposes of processing

The Company processes personal information only for the purposes below. If a purpose changes, the Company takes the steps the law requires, such as getting your consent in advance.

  • Membership sign-up and management: identity confirmation, confirming your intent to sign up, keeping you logged in, checking the age limit, and processing account deletion
  • Providing the dating service: creating and showing profiles, recommending members who match your preferences, interest requests and matching, chats, and setting up dates
  • Translation: translating chats and profiles between Korean, Vietnamese, and English
  • A safe environment: reviewing profiles and photos, handling reports and blocks, warning about risky chats (such as fraud, requests for money, or attempts to move the conversation to outside contact channels), restricting use, and preventing sanctioned members from signing up again
  • Customer support: receiving and answering inquiries, and handling disputes

4. Destruction procedure and method

  • Procedure: An automated job runs every hour. It selects information whose retention period has ended and destroys it.
  • Method: We delete information in the database so that it cannot be recovered, and we delete photo files from storage. We do not create paper documents.

5. Provision to third parties

The Company does not provide members' personal information to third parties. The exceptions are when a law allows it, or when an investigative agency requests it under procedures set by law.

The following information is visible to other members within the Service.

  • Profile: The profile and photos you enter are shown to members you are recommended to and members you match with. If you turn on "show photos only after matching", only your main photo is visible before matching.
  • Date safety sharing: If you send a sharing link to tell someone you know about a date, the person who receives the link can see the date time and place, and the names and verification status of both members. The link is shared only when you send it yourself. It no longer opens 1 day after the date time.

7. Automated processing and use of AI

  • Member recommendations: A program selects recommended members based on the conditions you enter, such as nationality, gender, preferred age, and hide settings. Recommendations are reference information to help you decide for yourself whether to send an interest request.
  • Photo review assistance: AI looks at profile photos and gives an opinion on whether they can be shown. The AI does not identify who the person in the photo is. An operator makes the final decision to approve a photo, ask for changes, or hide it.
  • Risky chat warnings: If a chat contains a sentence that appears to ask for money, push the conversation to outside contact channels, offer visa-related deals, or use explicit language, the recipient sees a warning on their screen. We do not sanction the sender based on this detection result alone.

The Company does not make decisions that significantly affect your rights or obligations by fully automated means without human involvement. If you want an explanation of the processing above or want to object to it, you can contact us at the address in section 12.

8. Your rights and how to exercise them

  • You can ask at any time to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to stop its processing. You can also withdraw your consent.
  • You can edit your profile and photos yourself on the Me screen in the app. You can delete your account in the app under Me → Delete account. For details, see Account Deletion.
  • For requests that are hard to handle in the app, email the contact in section 12 from the email address you signed up with. We will verify your identity and tell you the result within 10 days.
  • You can also exercise your rights through a legal representative or an authorized person. In that case, a power of attorney must be submitted.
  • Requests to delete information may be limited if other laws require us to keep it, or if it relates to another member's rights (for example, the other member's chat history).

9. Security measures

  • Administrative measures: We keep the number of operators who can access personal information to a minimum, and we log what operators do with it.
  • Technical measures: All communication is encrypted with HTTPS. We store login tokens as hashes, and devices keep them in secure storage. The database cannot be accessed directly from outside.
  • Physical measures: Our servers are in a cloud provider's data center with controlled access.

10. Automatic collection tools

The app and this website do not use cookies, advertising identifiers, or behavioral analytics tools. The app stores only the information needed to keep you logged in, in the device's secure storage.

11. No use by people under 19

Only adults aged 19 or older can use the Service. The Company does not knowingly collect personal information from people under 19. If we find out that we have, we delete that account and information without delay.
